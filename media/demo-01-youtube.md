# YouTube demo 01

## Title

LoopAuditioneer: From One Recording to Hauptwerk Samples | Demo 01

## Description

A first look at the modern LoopAuditioneer workspace: turn a full register recording into individual notes, find and compare sustain loops, adjust attacks and releases, and export WAVs with Hauptwerk-style filenames.

Save your progress as a register project and continue later. Everything runs locally, with original recordings preserved.

This demo also shows quick bulk approval for testing. Final samples still deserve a listening pass!

Code and setup: https://github.com/hwvm/LoopAuditioneer-newUI

Built alongside the original open-source LoopAuditioneer. Thanks to its authors and contributors.
